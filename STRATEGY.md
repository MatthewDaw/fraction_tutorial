# fraction-tutorial-ui Strategy

## Target problem

Students struggle to add arbitrary fractions because the topic is taught as a single procedure rather than a chain of composable sub-skills (primes, common factors, base equating, simplification). Without per-concept visibility, neither the student nor a teacher can tell *which* sub-skill is the actual blocker.

## Our approach

Decompose fraction addition into a chain of small, visual, interactive lessons, with per-concept assessment so weaknesses are pinpointed and drilled — rather than re-explaining the whole procedure.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Self-paced students learning fraction addition — they're hiring fraction-tutorial-ui to work through the sub-skills independently, drilling weak spots until they can confidently add any two fractions.

## Key metrics

- **Final-assessment pass rate** — % of students who can correctly add two arbitrary fractions on the capstone lesson.
- **Per-concept mastery rate** — for each sub-skill (primes, common factors, base equating, simplification), the % of students who reach the mastery threshold. Surfaces the weakest lesson in the chain.
- **Lesson completion rate** — % of students who start a lesson and finish it. A drop here signals a lesson is too hard, too long, or too dull.

## Tracks

### Lesson coverage

Build out the lesson chain to completion: base equating (next), then simplification (after common factors), then the capstone "add any two fractions" lesson.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The approach only works if the full chain of sub-skills exists — every missing lesson is a gap a student can't cross.

### Interactive & visual scaffolding

Invest in the in-lesson UX: visual aids, hands-on manipulation, and the interactive mechanics that let students play with the numbers rather than just read about them.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Decomposition alone isn't the differentiator — *interactive* decomposition is. Each lesson has to be something a student can poke at, not a slide deck.

### Assessment & weakness tracking

Build the per-concept testing and tracking layer that records mastery, identifies weak spots, and feeds targeted drilling back to the student.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Pinpointing weaknesses is half the approach. Without this layer, the lessons are just a linear textbook in a browser.
